FaceMaskAvatar

Edit faces in photos with avatars or mosaic effects

About FaceMaskAvatar

FaceMaskAvatar detects faces in your photos and lets you choose which faces to edit.

You can cover selected faces with avatar images or apply a circular mosaic effect. Faces you choose not to edit remain unchanged.

Edited photos can be saved to your photo library. Use the app when you want to cover faces before sharing photos.

Key Features

  • Automatic face detection in photos
  • Individual selection of faces to edit
  • Avatar overlays to cover selected faces
  • Circular mosaic effects on selected faces
  • Built-in avatar images
  • Import custom images from Photos or Files
  • Save edited photos to your photo library
  • Japanese and English language support

How to Use

1. Select a Photo

Choose one photo to edit. The app detects faces in the photo.

2. Select Faces to Edit

Tap detected faces to choose which ones to edit and which ones to leave unchanged.

3. Choose an Editing Method

Apply an avatar overlay or a mosaic effect to the selected faces.

4. Save Your Photo

Review the edited result and save it to your photo library.

Custom Images

In addition to the built-in avatar images, you can use images of your own.

Import images from Photos or Files and use them as custom avatars.

Photos and Privacy

Face detection and image processing take place on your device. The app's implementation does not include functionality to send your photos, face images, or custom images to external servers.

Custom images are stored within the app. Edited photos are saved to your photo library at your direction and with the required permission.

For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.
