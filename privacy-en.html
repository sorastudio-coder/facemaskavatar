FaceMaskAvatar

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FaceMaskAvatar handles photos, face data, custom images, and other information when you use the app.

2. Photos and Face Data

FaceMaskAvatar detects faces in photos selected by the user and allows the user to apply avatar overlays or mosaic effects to selected faces.

Data Processed and Purpose of Use

To process photos, the app processes face detection results, such as the locations and regions of faces in a photo. These results are used only to allow the user to select faces and apply avatar overlays or mosaic effects to the selected faces. The app is not designed to identify individuals through facial recognition.

Storage and Sharing with Third Parties

Face detection and image processing take place on the user's device. The app does not transmit face detection results or photos to external servers or provide them to third parties.

Retention and Deletion

Face detection results are used as needed for face selection and photo editing. They are not stored on external servers as face data. Any face detection results retained within the app are used to support its photo editing functions.

When the user chooses to save an edited image, it is saved as a separate photo in the device's photo library. The user can delete the edited photo from the photo library. The app does not intentionally overwrite or delete the original photo.

3. Custom Images

You may import your own images from Photos or Files to use as custom avatars.

Imported custom images are stored on your device within the app for use as custom avatars. The app does not transmit these images to external servers or provide them to third parties.

Custom images can be deleted using the management features provided by the app. The app does not intentionally modify or delete the original images imported from Photos or Files.

4. Information Collection and Sharing

The app is designed to process photos selected by the user, face detection results, and custom images locally on the device. The app does not include functionality to transmit these images or face detection results to external servers or provide them to third parties.

The app does not require an account or login.

5. Permissions

The app uses iOS photo access features to let you select images and save edited photos. Custom images can be imported from Photos or Files using the access provided by iOS and the items you select. Access is subject to the permissions and selections you make in iOS.

6. Children's Privacy

FaceMaskAvatar is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not include an account registration feature.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
